Unlike generic tools like Asana or Monday, Flowly speaks designer — with native Figma integration, visual timelines, and async-first communication.

## Voice & Tone

**Brand personality:**
Flowly is the smart, calm colleague who always has their act together — but never makes you feel disorganized by comparison. They're warm, capable, and speak plainly. They don't over-explain.

**Brand archetype:** The Creator — empowers others to produce their best work.

### Voice Pillars

**1. Clear**
What it means: We say exactly what we mean, in the fewest words needed.
Sounds like: "Your project is 3 days behind. Here's why."
Not: Dumbed-down or cold. Clear is respectful, not curt.

**2. Warm**
What it means: We care about the person behind the screen, not just the task.
Sounds like: "Nice work closing that sprint — your team shipped 9 stories."
Not: Sycophantic or hollow. Warmth has to be earned by context.

**3. Capable**
What it means: We project confidence without arrogance.
Sounds like: "Flowly syncs with Figma automatically — no setup required."
Not: Boastful. We let the product speak; we just make sure the story is clear.

### Tone Matrix

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| Marketing | Confident, energetic | "Ship better work, together." |
| Onboarding | Warm, guiding | "Let's get your first project set up — takes 2 minutes." |
| Error states | Calm, reassuring | "Something went wrong on our end. Your work is safe — we're on it." |
| Support | Empathetic, fast | "Got it. Let me pull up your account right now." |
| Social | Conversational, relatable | "Reminder that 'quick sync' is not a project plan. 👀" |

## Brand Audit: Example Findings

**Materials reviewed:** 3 social posts, homepage hero, onboarding email #1

**Overall consistency score: 6.5/10**

### Issues Found

**Social post (Jan 14):**
> "Flowly's AI-powered smart workflow optimization capabilities enable seamless cross-functional team synchronization."

Problem: Violates every voice pillar. Jargon-heavy, passive, says nothing real.
Fix: "Flowly keeps your team in sync — without the meetings." ✓

**Onboarding email, subject line:**
> "Welcome to Flowly! Get started with our platform today!"

Problem: Generic. Doesn't reinforce brand personality. Exclamation marks dilute voice.
Fix: "You're in. Let's build something great." ✓

**Homepage hero subheadline:**
> "The best project management solution for creative professionals looking to optimize their workflow."

Problem: Weak positioning. "Best" is unsubstantiated. "Looking to optimize" is passive.
Fix: "Built for design teams. Syncs with Figma. Ships projects on time." ✓

### Top 3 Priorities
1. **Purge jargon across all touchpoints** — "optimization," "seamless," "solution" don't belong in Flowly's voice.
2. **Rewrite onboarding email sequence** — Current emails read as generic SaaS. Opportunity to establish voice at the highest-intent moment.
3. **Add emotional payoff to marketing copy** — Features are listed but the *why it matters to Mia* is missing.
